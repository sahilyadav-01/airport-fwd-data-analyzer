ICAO ACR-PCR System

Computes the official ICAO ACR-PCR rating under FAA AC 150/5320-6G (the mandatory worldwide replacement for ACN-PCN).

ICAO Overload Criteria
• ACR ≤ PCR: Unrestricted operations.
• PCR < ACR ≤ 1.10 PCR: Permissible overload.
• ACR > 1.10 PCR: Restricted (risk of premature structural failure).
